Micro REPL turns your phone into a MicroPython IDE. Plug your board in over USB and get a live REPL, a file explorer and a code editor. No laptop required.

<b>Terminal</b>
<ul>
<li>A live MicroPython prompt: type code and see the result right away.</li>
<li>Step through your command history.</li>
<li>Soft reset the board, or stop a script that's stuck in a loop.</li>
</ul>

<b>Explorer</b>
<ul>
<li>Browse the files on your board.</li>
<li>Create, rename, delete and run files and folders.</li>
<li>Import scripts from your phone.</li>
</ul>

<b>Editor</b>
<ul>
<li>Python syntax highlighting, undo and redo, and line numbers.</li>
<li>Run a script on the board in one tap and watch its output.</li>
<li>Save to the board, or to any folder on your phone.</li>
</ul>

<b>And more</b>
<ul>
<li>Recent scripts: files you opened or saved come back in one tap.</li>
<li>20+ themes that colour the whole app, not just the code.</li>
<li>Pick the board to use from a list of connected USB devices.</li>
<li>Works on phones and tablets.</li>
</ul>

<b>What you need</b>
<ul>
<li>Android 7.0 or newer, with USB On-The-Go support.</li>
<li>A board flashed with MicroPython, such as a Raspberry Pi Pico or an ESP32.</li>
</ul>

Micro REPL is free and open source, and also runs on Windows, macOS, Linux and in the browser: https://github.com/Ma7moud3ly/micro-repl
